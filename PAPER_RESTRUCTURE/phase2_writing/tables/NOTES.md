## Package requirements
`booktabs` (`\toprule`, `\midrule`, `\bottomrule`, `\cmidrule`, `\addlinespace`), `multirow`,
`amsmath`/`amssymb` (`\mathbb`, `\text`). All four are already in the `main-tnse.tex` preamble.
No custom macros. `\setlength{\tabcolsep}{...}` is set inside each float, so it does not leak.

**Not compiled:** no LaTeX toolchain is installed in this environment. Each file was validated
structurally instead: column counts per row vs the `tabular` spec (including `\multicolumn` spans),
balanced braces, even `$` counts, matched `\begin`/`\end`. Compile once before assembly.

## Rounding applied
Q and Q-differences → 4 dp; δ, retention, γ → 3 dp; AMI/ARI/NMI → 3 dp; cluster counts → 1 dp;
hb → 3 dp; correlations and p-values → 3 dp. Negative signs use `$-$` for correct math minus.

## Caveats, per table

### 1. `tab_null_control.tex`
- **Real-arm stds are structurally zero** for `Q_fixed_base`, `delta`, `hb`: the real arm uses one
  Leiden partition, so those quantities have no replicate variance. Only `dQ_fixed` and
  `actual_retention` vary across the 3 sparsification seeds. Per the "± only where the CSV has it"
  rule, ± is printed only for `ΔQ_fixed`; the remaining stds are summarised as upper bounds in the
  caption (max over all 34 rows: Q_base 0.0013, δ 0.0036, hb 0.175, retention 0.0036).
- **δ at 3 dp loses the facebook detail**: facebook-combined real δ = −0.001364 prints as
  `−0.001`; com-Amazon real δ = 0.009612 prints as `+0.010`. Bump δ to 4 dp if §5.2 wants to lean
  on the exact facebook value.
- cit-Patents and wiki-topcats **null arms used reduced replication** (1 rewire seed × 2
  sparsification seeds instead of 2 × 2) — flagged in the caption, not by a symbol in the table.
- The "median ratio 1.24", "15/17", "16/17", "17/17" claims in the caption are taken from
  `exp_B_config_null/SUMMARY.md`, not recomputed here.

### 2. `tab_transfer_loss.tex`
- **No `Q_base` column exists in the source CSV.** The `ΔQ` column is the file's own
  `delta_Q_mean` (seeded-final minus base, computed inside `exp4_comprehensive_analysis.py`). The
  baseline itself cannot be printed from this file.
- **No std for `ΔQ` or for `Q_transfer_loss`** in the CSV (`delta_Q_std_reduction` is a *ratio of
  stds*, a different quantity, and is not shown). ± is printed only for `Q_sparse`, `Q_on_orig`,
  `Q_final`.
- **α = 1.0 rows excluded**, as instructed: the sampler ran on them (realized retention
  0.350–0.589 across the 15 datasets), so they are ~50%-retention runs, not baselines.
- Three near-zero ΔQ cells round to a signed zero at 4 dp: ca-CondMat α=0.8 (−4.97e−5 → `$-$0.0000`),
  facebook-combined α=0.8 (+1.72e−5 → `+0.0000`), cit-HepTh α=0.8 (+1.28e−5 → `+0.0000`). Left as is
  to keep the 4-dp rule; switch that column to units of 10⁻³ if the signed zero reads badly in print.
- **Dataset set differs from exp_B/exp_E**: exp4 has 15 datasets including `soc-Epinions1` and
  *excluding* `email-Eu-core`, `ca-GrQc`, `cit-Patents`, `wiki-topcats`. Tables 1, 2 and 4 are
  therefore not row-aligned; do not cross-reference them per network without checking membership.
- `m` is not in this CSV, so Table 2 has no edge-count column (Tables 1 and 4 do).
- Transfer loss over the printed rows ranges 0.0043 (facebook-combined α=0.9) to 0.1852
  (wiki-Talk α=0.8). The skeleton's "0.01–0.19" is a rounded restatement; the true minimum is
  0.0043, so the caption avoids quoting a range.

### 3. `tab_gt_recheck.tex`
- **Dolphins ground truth is a surrogate**: `exp_F/run.py::load_dolphins` builds it with
  `networkx.algorithms.community.kernighan_lin_bisection(G, seed=0)`, not published labels
  (the script itself flags this). Marked with `$^{\dagger}$` and a note line below the tabular
  (plain `\footnotesize` paragraph — no `threeparttable` dependency).
- Mean ± std computed over the 10 seeds with **population std (ddof = 0)**, which reproduces the
  numbers in `exp_F/SUMMARY.md` exactly (e.g. Karate baseline AMI 0.5573 ± 0.0281). Sample std
  would give 0.0297.
- Condition `resmatch_dspar_paper_08` is printed as "resolution-matched control"; its γ column is
  the mean of the per-seed tuned resolution (Karate 2.000, Dolphins 2.300, Football 1.000,
  Polbooks 1.500, email-Eu-core 1.075). Football's control degenerates to γ = 1.0, i.e. it is
  identical to the baseline row — that is real, not a copy-paste error.
- `true ret.` is the mean realized retention over the 10 seeds (1.000 for baseline and resmatch).
- The caption's "+0.014 vs +0.007" and "0/5" verdict come from `exp_F/SUMMARY.md`.

### 4. `tab_boundary.tex`
- Significance recomputed here as `y > 2 * Q_base_std` from `outcomes.csv`; it reproduces the
  `y>2sigma` column of `exp_E/SUMMARY.md` exactly — 2/15: email-Enron (+0.0077) and
  com-Youtube (+0.0086). Bold + `$^{\ast}$`.
- facebook-combined `Q_base_std` = 7.34e−5 prints as `0.0001` at 4 dp.
- `y` values are O(10⁻³); at 4 dp several rows print as `+0.0004`/`+0.0006` etc. Kept at 4 dp per
  the rounding rule.
- exp_E used 3 sparsification seeds vs exp_C's 5, so `Q_seeded` differs slightly from the exp_C
  table in `exp_C/SUMMARY.md` (|Δy| ≤ 0.0019, cross-checked in exp_E SUMMARY §1); the two tables
  should not be presented as identical measurements.

### 5. `tab_predictors.tex`
- The CSV contains 14 predictors × 2 outcomes = **28 tests**; both outcomes are shown (`y` =
  runtime-matched, `y2` = naive), so the reader can see the naive outcome is what produces the two
  nominally significant cells (hb excess vs y2, p = 0.026; n vs y2, p = 0.044).
- **No corrected p-values in the CSV.** The caption's "neither survives correction for the 28
  tests" is an inference from the reported raw p-values (min p = 0.026 > 0.05/28), not a column
  read from the file. Reword if a formal correction is run later.
- The wiki-Talk collapse figures (r 0.69 → 0.12 for deg_cv) are quoted from `exp_E/SUMMARY.md`,
  not from `correlations.csv` (which holds only the full-sample values).
- Predictors are ordered as in the CSV.

### 6. `tab_sampler_audit.tex`
- This is the only **assembled** table; it pools measured `actual_retention` across files rather
  than reading one CSV. Ranges are min–max of the per-network mean realized retention:
  - `paper` α=0.8, n=54 = exp4 (15 datasets) + exp_B (17 networks × real and null arms = 34) +
    exp_F (5 small graphs, per-dataset mean over 10 seeds) → 0.317–0.549.
  - `paper` α=0.9 (0.334–0.554) and α=1.0 (0.350–0.589): exp4 only, 15 datasets.
  - `probabilistic_no_replace` α=0.8 (0.400–0.619) and α=0.9 (0.432–0.660): exp_C, 6 datasets.
  - `calibrated` α=0.8 (0.799–0.800): exp_C, 6 datasets. α=0.9 (0.887–0.901): exp_C (6) + exp_E
    (15) + exp_F (5) = 26. The 0.887 low end is Karate (n=34), where the retention grid is coarse.
- **`probabilistic_no_replace` at α=1.0 is a single number, not a range**: 0.662 on email-Eu-core,
  documented in `AUDIT_FINDINGS.md` §4 / `exp_C/SUMMARY.md` §0. No other network was run at that
  setting, so `# = 1`.
- **`calibrated` at α=1.0 is `--`**: never run (α=1.0 is the no-sparsification sentinel). It is
  1.000 by construction, but that value is not on disk, so it is not printed.
- exp_C also ran α ∈ {0.7, 0.95}; those ranges are given in the caption rather than as extra rows,
  to keep the requested α ∈ {0.8, 0.9, 1.0} row structure.
- The "13–35% of edges hit the clip" figure in the caption is from `exp_C/SUMMARY.md` §0.

## Cells left as `--`
